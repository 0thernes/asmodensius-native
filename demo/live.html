What you are looking at

One world of the Asmodensius-Native engine (C23 with assembly kernels, Windows), running in the program az_live. Every picture is made by the engine's own software renderer (view/az_view.c) between ticks. This page only shows the pictures and the numbers the engine prints. It never changes the world. Each picture line carries the state hash taken before and after drawing, and the two are compared.

Wild and lab

Wild is the default: the world is made of fresh entropy from the operating system and draws 16 more bytes every tick. It has no seed, and no two runs are the same. Lab is the seeded test bench: the same seed gives the same world, for tests and lesion contrasts. In both modes the total of matter and the total of energy are compared with their starting values after every tick.
