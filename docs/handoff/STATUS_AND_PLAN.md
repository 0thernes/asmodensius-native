## 9. Limits that do not go away

- 1,000,000 creatures cannot be cache-resident: the hot tier alone is 140 MB at that size, and the machine has 40 MB of L2 and 36 MB of L3. The engine streams 128-creature blocks to keep each block in cache.
- Large pages are unavailable (the account lacks the lock-memory privilege); the arena is locked with VirtualLock instead.
- By your rules L6 and L7 the engine runs only as a Windows program from Z:, and "below assembly" means what a Windows program can reach: machine code, the microarchitecture (ports, micro-op cache, prefetch, cache sets, TLB), core placement, locked memory and the GPU driver API. Microcode, model-specific registers and cache partitioning stay out of reach without a kernel driver; gates, transistors and lithography are research background, not something code can change.
- Checked 2026-11-17: the account is an administrator but the session is not elevated; "Lock pages in memory" is not granted (no large pages); llvm-mca (LLVM 21, knows arrowlake-s) and Windows Performance Recorder are present; nvdisasm, cuobjdump and ptxas (CUDA toolkit) are not; the active power plan is "CQM Ultimate Performance".
- The "quantum" kernels are classical integer simulations of small state vectors; they give no quantum speed-up.
- Consciousness lists are indicator-only: passing an indicator is not evidence of consciousness.
