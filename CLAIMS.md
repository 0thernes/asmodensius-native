# Claims Ledger (`CLAIMS.md`)

Every substantive technical or scientific claim made for `Asmodensius-Native` v1.2.0 is listed below with its epistemic tier, verification command, primary evidence artifact, and explicit falsification condition (what would prove us wrong).

## Epistemic Tiers

- **`kernel-checked`:** Machine-checked locally on Windows by 6 checkers (`lean4` kernel, `leanchecker`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`, `nanoda_bin`) and in Ubuntu CI also by `lean4lean` (`lake check --paranoid`) and `lake comparator --paranoid`, strictly within `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. These claims concern the hand-written `Nat` definitions in `lean/AsmodensiusProofs/Spec.lean`, **not** the C23, NASM, or CUDA PTX code.
- **`program-audited`:** Verified deterministically by automated test binaries, binary-inspection gates, static linters, or sanitizers against the current commit.
- **`empirical`:** Measured on specific named hardware under documented flags (`--lab`, `locked=1`); valid for that binary, topology, and seed configuration.
- **`indicator-only`:** Diagnostic or cataloging instrument that measures mechanistic properties without claiming sentience, consciousness, biological life, or AGI (Law 8).
- **`open-conjecture`:** Unresolved scientific question; explicitly NOT claimed solved or retired.
