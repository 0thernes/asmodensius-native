# Code of Conduct (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to keep participation in `Asmodensius-Native` respectful, constructive, evidence-driven, and harassment-free for everyone regardless of background, experience level, or affiliation.

## Expected Behavior

- Critique code, proofs, and empirical methodology rigorously and politely -- never attack people.
- Uphold scientific honesty: report negative results, limitations, and failed replications clearly without exaggeration.
- Respect project scope and Law 8 (no fabricated claims of sentience, consciousness, or retired open problems).

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing private personal information of others.
- Deliberate fabrication of benchmarks, citations, or verification results.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to `0_0@0thernes.art`. Maintainers may remove off-topic or abusive issues and restrict participation when necessary.
